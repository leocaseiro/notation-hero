Aligning the shared vocabulary before we touch the wireframe. Instrument-agnostic (drums · piano · guitar · …). Nothing here is decided — it's a playback of your model so we can point at boxes and fix names.

The one big idea. Everything you can press ▶ on is one of two things: a leaf = a single piece of Notation (a score),  or  a sequence = ordered Steps, each pointing at another playable.

Song, Lesson, Pattern, Part are just roles on that single shape (now all called a Playable) — which is why a lesson, a groove, and a structured song-tutorial all feel the same. They are.

And every Playable is scored on its own. A Lesson has its own score from playing the whole thing (the "final exam") — independent of its steps. Skip the steps, play the lesson, score 100%. No averaging.
