NotationHero — Player UI

Tablet-landscape. "Calm by default, everything one tap away." Pick the in-play HUD default (D-4a) and a Results direction (D-4b). Feedback on the staff uses the locked language: ring + chevron, perfect 🟢 / early 🟠 / late 🟣 / extra red ✗.

Play screen — Minimal (proposed default)

Default view: notation + transport + a tiny combo, and a row of panel chips you tap to reveal Score / Kit / Timeline / Mixer. Nothing shouts. The chips are the answer to "what's toggleable."

Only the few notes you just hit carry a mark (🟢 perfect, 🟠 early ‹, 🟣 late ›, red ✗ extra). The blue line is the playhead. Combo sits quiet, top-right.

Results screen — two directions (D-4b)

Both carry everything: 0-100%, 5-star, Perfect/Good/OK/Miss tiers, streak. They differ in feel. This is a dedicated screen, so density is free here.
