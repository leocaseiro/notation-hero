2 · Three in-notehead approaches

Each shows the full 5-state language on real drum noteheads (snare oval + cymbal X). Extra/wrong-hit is always a red ✗ at the wrongly-hit staff position — different shape AND place, so it never relies on color. Miss = the notehead just dims (no mark).

⌖ Why this is accessible: color is never the only signal. Perfect/early/late are rings or fills on the correct note; extra is a cross at a different position; early vs late differ by which side the mark sits (left/right) — readable even in greyscale. The color-blind-safe palette is a one-tap swap on top.

4 · Or keep the staff totally clean — direction in the HUD

The lightest-clutter option: on the staff, a hit note gets only a colored ring (perfect/early/late by color + shape, no direction mark). The precise rush/drag reading lives off-staff in a tendency meter + combo glow — a toggleable HUD panel, not on the notation. Best when you want the score to stay pristine.
